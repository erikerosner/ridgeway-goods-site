01About

ridgeway goods is a small apparel company based in Charlotte, North Carolina. We make brands with a point of view and hold every one of them to the same standard.

The standard

A garment worth wearing with nothing printed on it. Design that is drawn, spaced and second-guessed until it lands. Nothing made until someone orders it.

The approach

Each brand gets its own voice and its own audience. The systems behind them are shared, so every new one starts with the lessons of the last.
