01 / About

Ridgeway Goods is a husband and wife apparel company started from the corner office of our home in Charlotte, North Carolina.

We’re dedicated to building relatable brands that sell quality products. One of us is a trained graphic designer, and we both come from families of artists and entrepreneurs, so making things and selling them has always felt like the family business. Ridgeway Goods is where those skills meet the things we love.

  • Approachable

    Designed for the people who live the thing, not just the ones who already dress the part.

  • Affordable

    Good design shouldn’t cost a premium. We keep prices within reach of everyone.

  • Elevated

    We’re here to raise the bar for design and quality in categories that usually settle for less.

How it’s different from a group order. A group order puts an existing design on a lot of shirts. Custom starts with the design itself. Longer timeline, more back and forth, and something nobody else has at the end.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need.

    Fill out the form: quantity, timeline, and what you’re picturing. The more you give us up front, the faster we come back with real numbers instead of more questions.

  2. 2

    We come back with a plan.

    Usually within two business days. Scope, timeline, and actual pricing, not a range that means nothing. If your date is too tight, we’ll tell you then rather than halfway through.

  3. 3

    We make it.

    Group orders go into production as soon as you approve. Custom work starts with concepts, and nothing gets printed until you’ve seen them.
