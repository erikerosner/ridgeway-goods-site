Privacy

How we handle your information

Ridgeway Goods LLC is a husband and wife apparel company in Charlotte, North Carolina. This page explains what we collect on ridgeway-goods.com, why, who helps us handle it, and how to ask us to change or delete it. Questions go to help@ridgeway-goods.com.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version

  • We only ask for what we use, and we tell you why when we ask.
  • We never sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • No tracking cookies and no ad pixels.
  • We only email you if you say yes, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Ask us anytime to see, change or delete what we have, and we will.

01

The brand survey

When you take the survey at ridgeway-goods.com/vote/, we save:

  • Your answers: the niches you picked and how you ranked them, the pieces you'd buy, and anything you typed into "Something else".
  • Your email address. It's the one thing we require, so each person's vote counts once. If you send the survey again with the same email, your new answers replace the old ones.
  • Only if you choose to add them: your first name, your city and state, your gender, your age range, and whether you've shopped with us before. We use these to understand who wants what, so we get sizes, fits and locations right.
  • A few details that come with every response: when you sent it, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer), how long the survey took, and which of our links brought you (for example, our email or a QR code on a Pack of Pickles order). These are our own labels. We don't follow you around the web to get them.

While you're taking the survey, your browser keeps your answers until you close the tab, so a refresh doesn't lose them. Nothing is sent to us until you press Submit my vote.

02

Email updates

At the end of the survey we ask whether you'd like to hear what wins. If you say yes, we add your email (and first name, if you gave it) to our email list, along with labels for the niches you picked, so we can send you news about the niches you care about. We'll email you about new niches, brands and stores from Ridgeway Goods. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect right away.

If you say no, we don't add you to any list. Your vote still counts.

03

Light and dark mode, and your city

The site switches between light and dark at sunrise and sunset where you are. To do that, it asks Cloudflare for the approximate city your internet connection comes from. That city is kept in your browser for 7 days so the site doesn't have to ask again. We don't store it. On the survey, we may show that city as a suggestion for "Where you are". It's only saved with your answers if you tap it.

The header shows Charlotte's time and weather from the National Weather Service. That involves no information about you.

04

Who helps us

We use a few services to run the site. Each one only gets what it needs for its job:

  • Cloudflare runs the small program that receives your survey answers, and its Turnstile check tells people apart from bots without puzzles. To do that, Turnstile looks at technical details about your browser and connection.
  • Google stores the survey answers in a private Google Sheet that only we can open.
  • Kit runs our email list, and only gets your details if you say yes to updates.
  • GitHub hosts the website. Like any web host, it may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Typeform still runs our contact and custom order forms, for now. What you send through those forms is handled by Typeform under its own privacy policy.

Our brand stores, such as Pack of Pickles, have their own privacy policies on their own sites.

05

How long we keep it

  • Survey answers: until we've made our decision and for up to two years after the survey closes. Then we delete them.
  • Email list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.

06

Your choices

Email help@ridgeway-goods.com to see what we have about you, correct it, or delete it. We'll reply within 30 days. To stop emails, use the unsubscribe link in any of them.

07

Children

The survey is for ages 13 and up. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

08

Keeping it safe

Everything you send travels over an encrypted connection. Your answers are kept in accounts that only we can open.

09

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
